Security your IT team will sign off on.

Your pricing and product data are among your most sensitive information. We treat them that way, and we prove it.

Our SOC 2 Type 2 report

System and Organization Controls (SOC) reports are examinations defined by the AICPA and carried out by independent CPA firms. Flow's SOC 2 Type 2 report covers our controls relevant to security, availability and confidentiality, and tests that they worked throughout the period, not just that they're written down.

For you, that means the security questions your IT and legal teams would ask have already been answered and checked by a third party.
