We clean your data.

One complete, correct record for every part you sell, built from what you already publish. No PIM or data team needed on your side.

  • Every orderable part number, with families and variants.
  • Duplicates merged and conflicting values flagged for your team to confirm once.
  • Dimensions, weights, UPCs and pack quantities filled from your spec sheets.
  • Classified to ETIM, the industry standard, so your products show up when a buyer filters by IP65, 3000 K or 0–10 V.
  • Crosses that work. Standard attributes let reps cross a competitor's part straight into yours.
  • Every value traceable to the sentence on your spec sheet it came from.
